WHO WE ARE

More Than a Product.
It's a Purpose.

ReLeaf is a student-led business initiative focused on promoting eco-friendly everyday products. Through notebooks, bookmarks and paper bags, we aim to encourage thoughtful choices and environmental awareness.

🌱 Our Mission

To make sustainable choices simple, useful and accessible.
